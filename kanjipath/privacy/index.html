KanjiPath: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · App: KanjiPath (com.zyncrologic.kanjipath) · Developer: Zyncrologic

In short
  • Everything you study stays on your phone. We have no servers and never receive your data.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking.
  • The one-time "KanjiPath Full" purchase is handled by Google Play.
  • Handwriting recognition downloads a model from Google once; Google's ML Kit library sends Google technical diagnostics.
  • If Android backup is on, your study progress can be included in your Google backup.

1. What the app keeps on your phone

The app keeps your study progress (lessons, review schedule, answers and practice results), your favourites and custom decks, and your settings in its private storage on your phone. The kanji dictionary, stroke order data and example sentences come with the app.

We never see this data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

2. Backups

Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include your study progress and settings in your Google account backup and restore them on a new phone. The dictionary and the handwriting model are not included. This backup is run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Backup files you make. Settings → Backup saves a file to a place you choose and can restore it later. We never see these files.

3. Handwriting

Stroke-by-stroke writing practice is checked by the app itself on your phone.

Free-draw practice and draw-to-search use Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. The first time you use them, ML Kit downloads Google's Japanese handwriting model (about 24 MB). After that, recognition runs on your phone, and the app does not send your handwriting to us or to Google.

Like all ML Kit features, the library sends limited technical data to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information (such as manufacturer, model and OS version), app package name and version, a per-installation identifier, performance metrics, API configuration, the size of the input and output, error codes, and, for handwriting recognition, the configured language. Google encrypts this data in transit and does not share it with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Japanese audio

To read Japanese aloud, the app passes the text to your phone's own text-to-speech engine. If a Japanese voice is missing, the app opens your phone's settings so you can install one. Text-to-speech engines and voice downloads are provided by your phone's maker or Google and governed by their terms.

5. Purchases

The app is free to use for the beginner levels. "KanjiPath Full" is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or bank details. The app checks your purchase with Google Play on your phone; we do not run any purchase server. See the Google Privacy Policy.

The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review. Google handles your rating and review.

6. Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules a notification on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not show ads or include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not upload your study progress, answers or handwriting.

8. Permissions

  • Notifications: daily study reminders, only if you turn them on.
  • Internet and network state: only for Google Play purchases, the one-time handwriting model download and the ML Kit diagnostics described above.
  • Run at startup, wake lock and foreground service: used by Android's background-task system to keep your reminder scheduled after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play billing: for the one-time purchase.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

10. Your choices and data deletion

Because we do not keep any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete. You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it or clearing its storage. Delete any backup files you made yourself, and manage your Google account backup in your Google account settings.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
