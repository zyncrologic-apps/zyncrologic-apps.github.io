BlockLevel: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · App: BlockLevel (com.zyncrologic.blocklevel) · Developer: Zyncrologic

In short
  • We collect no data. The app has no internet access at all.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking.
  • Your vehicles, settings and phone calibration stay on your phone.
  • If Android backup is on, they can be included in your Google backup.

1. What the app keeps on your phone

The app keeps the vehicles you add (name, type and measurements), your settings and your phone's calibration in its private storage on your phone. It reads your phone's motion sensor only while the level or calibration screen is open, and never stores or sends those readings.

We never see this data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

2. Backups

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include your vehicles and settings in your Google account backup and restore them on a new phone (the phone calibration is not reused there, because it belongs to the old phone). This backup is run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

3. Voice guidance

To speak instructions such as "Right wheels, 2 blocks", the app passes that text to your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Text-to-speech engines are provided by your phone's maker or Google and governed by their terms.

4. Purchase

BlockLevel is a paid app with a one-time price, processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or bank details. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not show ads or include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • The app cannot send anything anywhere: it does not have Android's internet permission.

6. Permissions

None. The app needs no Android permissions. It requires a motion sensor (accelerometer), which apps can read without a permission.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Your choices and data deletion

Because we do not keep any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete. You can delete everything the app stores with Settings → Reset everything, by uninstalling it, or by clearing its storage. Manage your Google account backup in your Google account settings.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
