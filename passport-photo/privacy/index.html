Passport Photo: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: Passport Photo (com.zyncrologic.passportphoto) · Developer: Zyncrologic

In short
  • Your photos are processed on your phone and are never uploaded by us.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Google's on-device face detection library sends technical diagnostics (never photos) to Google.

1. Your photos

The app uses your camera to show a live preview and to take your photo. You can also choose a photo from your gallery using the system photo picker. All checking, cropping and resizing happens on your phone.

We never upload, store on a server, or see your photos. Photos leave your phone only if you choose to share or save them using your phone's share menu (for example to your gallery, a printer, email or a messaging app). What happens then is governed by the app or service you choose.

Photos and files the app creates are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

2. Face detection on your phone

To guide you and check your photo, the app uses Google ML Kit face detection, which runs entirely on your phone. Your camera frames and photos are not sent to Google.

Like all ML Kit features, the library sends limited technical data to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information (such as manufacturer, model and OS version), app package name and version, performance metrics, API configuration (such as image size and format), error codes, and a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you or your device. Google encrypts this data in transit and does not share it with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases

The app is free to try. Saving photos requires a one-time in-app purchase, processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app, along with basic device and app information needed to process purchases. It does not receive your photos, name or email. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not show ads or include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use AI to edit your face or background. The app only crops and resizes.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: to take your photo and give live guidance.
  • Internet: only for in-app purchases and the ML Kit diagnostics described above.

Gallery import uses the system photo picker, so the app cannot see your other photos.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13. Parents and guardians may use it to take a child's passport photo; those photos stay on the parent's phone like any other photo.

7. Your choices and data deletion

Because we do not keep your photos or personal details, there is nothing for us to delete about you. You can delete all photos and files the app created by uninstalling it. To ask RevenueCat to delete purchase records linked to your anonymous app user ID, email us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

Passport Photo is not affiliated with any government agency. Final acceptance of a photo is decided by the issuing authority.
